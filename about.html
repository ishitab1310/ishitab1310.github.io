I'm Ishita — a 4th year Dual Degree student at IIIT Hyderabad doing B.Tech Computer Science and MS Computational Linguistics. I'm slow to warm up to people, but once you break through that shell, I'm very talkative. I can chat about literally anything, and I try to live life to the fullest. It's better to just do it anyway — you might not remember the embarrassing moments, but regrets are hard to forget.

I appreciate the sheer beauty of being alive. How wild it is that I get to exist in this world — and how much greater I can make it for people who come after me. That's the kind of thing that keeps me up at night, in a good way.

I use humour as a coping mechanism and casual roasting as my language of affection. ESFP, sometimes introvert. Powered by infinite coffees, pending deadlines, and very random playlists. Trying to debug my life and my code for the past four years — progress on both is ongoing.
